# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone. Be specific about technical
claims, critique ideas rather than people, assume good faith while requiring evidence, and respect
privacy when sharing production cases or incidents.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information,
or sustained disruption. Repository maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed promptly and as
confidentially as practical.
